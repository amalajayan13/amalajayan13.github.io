About Me

I am a PhD scholar in cybersecurity at Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham, India, and a member of the Applied Electromagnetics and Telecommunications Research Group (EMAT) at Université de Moncton, Canada. From March to August 2026 I was a visiting research student at the Mohamed bin Zayed University of Artificial Intelligence (MBZUAI), UAE.

My research is on intrusion detection that security teams can trust and deploy: models that explain their decisions, run on resource-constrained devices, and preserve evidence that holds up in a forensic investigation. Recent work covers transformer-based detection of network intrusions and DDoS traffic, tamper-evident alert logging, and federated detection across heterogeneous IoT devices. I hold B.Sc. and M.Sc. degrees in Cyber Forensics from Mahatma Gandhi University, Keralam, where I was a university position holder, and I have qualified the UGC-NET in Computer Science and Applications.

I welcome research collaborations, academic partnerships and professional opportunities. Please get in touch by email.
